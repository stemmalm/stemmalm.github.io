Collation

One line of Ω, as the three copyists wrote it, and what the children later said.

Read it as an edition reads manuscripts. Ω is the archetype; #1, #2 and #3 are the witnesses, each a copy written by that individual at 72 months. A reading that differs from Ω is a variant. Under the table, the apparatus lists every place where the witnesses disagree. Below it, the reception: the children who grew up on a copy, saying the pair that copy brought in.
