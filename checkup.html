Check-up

The same minimal pairs, put to every snapshot of every individual.

Each pair differs in one word. A snapshot passes a pair when it prefers the right line more than it did at 0 months, before it had heard anything; 0 months is 50% by definition. Dashed line: chance. Dotted line: 80%, the pass mark.

After 72 months #1–#3 heard children's books only; their clones #10–#12 heard books with family speech mixed back in (30%). #4–#9 live to 72 months. Exploratory; items written for this check-up, after Berko (1958) for the made-up words.
