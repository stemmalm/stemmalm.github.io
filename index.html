how to read
  1. Each bar is one life, left to right on one clock. Its colour is the chosen check-up, month by month.
  2. At 72 months a parent copies the book it heard, word by word, with 5% lapses; its children grow up on that copy.
  3. The thin line under a bar is what the individual was hearing; its colour is the same everywhere on the site.
  4. For the family in view, a band runs from the parent to each child; its width is how often the child says the parent's own lapses.
