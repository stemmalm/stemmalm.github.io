StemmaLM

A collection of small language-model lineages, raised under controlled conditions on corpora selected by features and size. Each model has a number, a record of what it heard and how it was trained, and snapshots that can be measured again. A grown model can copy what it heard, with slips, and raise the next generation on its copy.

The collection

Every model descends from one of three root corpora. Most of the collection is still a row of controlled conditions under those roots; #1 and #2 are the only models with children so far. A dashed line is a clone. Click a number for its record.
